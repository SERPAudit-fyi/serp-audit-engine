## Overview

SERP Audit Engine is an AI-powered SEO and search visibility audit framework that helps businesses identify website issues and improve their visibility across traditional search and AI platforms.

**Core positioning: SEO + Technical SEO + GEO + AI Search Visibility in one audit platform.**

---

## Key Areas

### SEO & Website Audits
Comprehensive on-page and off-page SEO audit workflows.

### Technical SEO Analysis
Crawlability, indexability, Core Web Vitals, schema, and site structure analysis.

### GEO Audits
Geographic and local search visibility assessment.

### AI Search Visibility
Visibility scoring across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot.

### AI Visibility Scoring
Structured scoring of brand and content presence in AI search responses.

### Content & On-Page Analysis
Content quality, keyword alignment, and on-page optimisation.

## Audit Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| seo-audit | Full on-page and off-page SEO website audit |
| technical-seo | Technical SEO crawlability, indexability, and CWV |
| geo-audit | Geographic and local search visibility audit |
| ai-visibility | AI search platform visibility and scoring |
